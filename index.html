Before You Apply

What you'll want to know

Is there a cost to attend?

No delegate fee. Seats are allocated by application, not payment — this keeps the room a peer room, not a paid audience.

What does an invitation include?

Two nights' stay and all meals in Goa for confirmed attendees. Travel to Goa is not included.

What happens after I apply?

Our team reviews every application against sector mix, mandate and fit, and confirms directly with your office — never a mass acceptance email.

What happens to the problem I submit?

Shared only with the small group matched to it in advance. Never published or attributed outside the room.

Only 50 seats. Applications close 31 August.
