Lot 06 — The Right Room

We don't want the biggest

room. We want the right one.

Approximately 50 seats. Every applicant is reviewed against the criteria below before an invitation is sent.

This room is for

  • CSOs, Heads of Sustainability, or equivalent mandate-holders
  • Organisations with a live, board-approved sustainability target
  • Leaders willing to table a real, unresolved problem

This room is not for

  • Vendors, consultants, or agencies selling into the space
  • Communications or PR-led attendees without execution mandate
  • Anyone unwilling to operate under the Chatham House Rule
Lot 07 — Before You Apply

What you'll want to know first.

What does an invitation include?

Two nights' stay and all meals in Goa are covered for confirmed attendees. Travel to Goa is not included.

Is there a cost to attend?

There is no delegate fee. Seats are allocated by application, not by payment — this keeps the room a peer room, not a paid audience.

How are applications reviewed?

Every application is checked against title, mandate, and sector, and confirmed by our team directly with your office.

What happens to what I submit for The Trash Table?

Your problem is shared only with the small group matched to it in advance, and never published outside the room.
